Who spawns, and how often

A share is the odds that the next animal the spawner makes, in one category, biome and season, is this one. It is the mix of picks. It is not a head count. Every biome counts the same, however big it is.

1. Which animals are common or rare?

One row per animal. The bar is its median share across the biomes that list it. The line runs from its smallest to its largest share. Red means it takes over somewhere (median 25% or more, or 50% or more in one biome). Grey means it never gets past 3% anywhere. "barred" counts biomes where the rules block it in this season.

2. What does one biome look like through the year?

Each bar is 100% of that biome's spawns in the category. Each animal keeps its colour across seasons, so you can watch it grow or shrink.
